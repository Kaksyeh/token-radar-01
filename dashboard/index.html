How the value is priced

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth. The category multiple is the median market-cap-per-TVL of the live-token protocols in that category (the global median where a category has fewer than 3 live protocols; clamped to 0.05–1.50). Growth clamps the 7-day TVL change to −50…+100% and counts 40% of it. Maturity rises with the protocol's age up to 3 years. Breadth adds 2% per extra chain, to 10%. Nothing here is a hardcoded list of names: the same formula scores every row, and the live-token set that calibrates it is shown on the Live tab.
